About the Retreat

A Weekend to Renew, Reflect & Connect

The Women of Purpose Conference is a transformative event set to unfold in January 2027 at Camp Copass in Denton, Texas. Nestled in serene surroundings, you'll have the opportunity to unwind by the lake and bask in the natural splendor, all while immersing yourself in learning and spiritual rejuvenation.

Friday evening's Girl Talk session features a panel ready to address your questions. Saturday is packed with enjoyable activities and personalized breakout sessions. The retreat closes Sunday with a serene morning prayer and hearty breakfast before transitioning to worship at Purpose Church of Denton.

Spots are limited to 28. Secure yours now for $150, or hold your spot with a non-refundable $50 deposit due by November 1, 2026. Balance is collected at check-in.
