About the Retreat

A weekend to empower, instruct, and stir up your God's given gifts.

The Assignment Retreat is a transformative event set to unfold in January 2027 at Camp Copass (Faith Dorm), 8200 E McKinney St, Denton, TX 76208. Nestled in serene surroundings, you'll have the opportunity to unwind by the lake and bask in the natural splendor, all while immersing yourself in learning and spiritual rejuvenation.

Friday evening's Girl Talk session features a panel ready to address your questions. Saturday is a full day of general sessions, breakout sessions, and activities by the lake. The retreat closes Sunday with a serene morning intercessory prayer and continental breakfast before transitioning to worship at Purpose Church of Denton, 3401 E University Dr #101, Denton, TX 76208.

Spots are limited to 28. Secure yours now for $150, or hold your spot with a non-refundable $50 deposit due by November 1, 2026. The remaining balance is due no later than January 8, 2027. We are not taking payments at the door.

Important Information

The conference is created to encourage, empower, and support you in a loving environment. We want you to feel welcomed and relaxed, so that you are ready to receive all the Lord has planned for this weekend. With that being said please understand the accommodations take place at a camp, so we will be comfortable indoors with beds and showers but you will need to provide your own bed linens and toiletries. The beds are bunk style but they are full size mattresses so you will still sleep comfortably.

Please bring the following:

  • Towels
  • Bed sheets, pillow, and pillowcase
  • Blanket or comforter
  • Travel toiletries (shampoo, soap, toothbrush, etc.)
  • Shower shoes
  • Comfortable tennis shoes
