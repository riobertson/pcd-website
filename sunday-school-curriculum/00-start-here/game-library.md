# Game Library: Reusable Games for Any Phase

These games work with **any phase**. Swap in that month's book names, verses, people or trivia. Each phase file also has its own story-specific games.

**Key:** 🐑 Little Lambs (4–6) · 🧭 Explorers (7–10) · 🥾 Trailblazers (11–13) · 🕊️ Ambassadors (14–17) · 🏃 Active · 🪑 Seated

---

## A. Book-Name Memory Games

### 1. Bible Book Shuffle 🧭🥾🕊️ 🏃
- **Supplies:** One set of book-name flashcards per team (this phase's books, or all books so far)
- **How to play:** Shuffle each set. On "Go!" teams race to lay the cards out **in Bible order**. The first correct team wins.
- **Younger:** Use only 3–5 cards with picture symbols. **Older:** Use all books learned so far, or mix in two sets and make them separate OT from NT.
- **Learning goal:** Book order

### 2. Missing Book 🐑🧭🥾 🪑
- **Supplies:** Flashcards on the floor or a pocket chart
- **How to play:** Lay the cards in order. Kids close their eyes while the teacher removes one. "Open!" The first to name the missing book gets a point (or gets to be the next "remover").
- **Younger:** 3–4 cards. **Older:** Remove 2–3 at once.
- **Learning goal:** Order + recall

### 3. Book Hop (Hopscotch) 🐑🧭 🏃
- **Supplies:** Painter's tape squares on the floor, each with a book name
- **How to play:** Kids hop square to square, saying each book as they land. If a kid misses a book, they start again.
- **Learning goal:** Order + movement memory

### 4. Before & After 🧭🥾🕊️ 🪑
- **How to play:** The teacher names a book, and kids name the book **before** and **after** it. ("Ruth!" → "Judges and 1 Samuel!")
- **Learning goal:** Deep order mastery

### 5. Pass the Bible (Hot Potato) 🐑🧭🥾 🏃
- **Supplies:** A soft Bible-shaped pillow or ball, and music
- **How to play:** Pass the object while music plays. When the music stops, the holder says the next book in the list (or answers a trivia question).
- **Gentle version:** Nobody is out. The holder can "phone a friend."
- **Learning goal:** Recall under fun pressure

### 6. Section Sort (Four Corners) 🧭🥾🕊️ 🏃
- **Supplies:** Section signs taped in corners (e.g., Law / History / Poetry / Prophets)
- **How to play:** Call out a book name, and kids run to its section corner.
- **Learning goal:** Sections of the Bible

### 7. Book Name Bowling 🐑🧭 🏃
- **Supplies:** 10 plastic bottles labeled with book names and a soft ball
- **How to play:** Knock pins down, then say (or spell) the names on the fallen pins to score.
- **Learning goal:** Recognition + reading

### 8. Bible Book Line-Up 🧭🥾🕊️ 🏃
- **Supplies:** One book-name card per child (pinned to shirt or held)
- **How to play:** Without talking (gestures only!), kids line up in Bible order. Time them and try to beat the record each week.
- **Learning goal:** Order + teamwork

### 9. Sword Drill 🧭🥾🕊️ 🪑
- See the full rules in [books-of-the-bible-memory-system.md](books-of-the-bible-memory-system.md#6-finding-books-in-a-real-bible-sword-drills).
- **Learning goal:** Finding books and verses quickly

## B. Spelling Games

### 10. Spell It Out Relay 🧭🥾🕊️ 🏃
- **Supplies:** Whiteboard or paper on a wall, markers, two or more teams in lines
- **How to play:** The teacher calls a book. The first player runs and writes the **first letter**, runs back, and tags the next player, who writes the second letter, and so on. The first correct team scores.
- **Younger helpers:** Little Lambs on a team write the first letter (with help).
- **Learning goal:** Spelling

### 11. Letter Scramble Race 🧭🥾 🪑
- **Supplies:** Letter tiles or cut-up letter cards in envelopes (one envelope per book)
- **How to play:** Teams unscramble the letters to build the book name. The first correct team wins.
- **Learning goal:** Spelling + recognition

### 12. Human Spelling 🧭🥾🕊️ 🏃
- **Supplies:** Large letter cards, one per child
- **How to play:** Call a book name. The kids holding those letters run up and stand in the right order to spell it.
- **Learning goal:** Spelling + teamwork

### 13. Spelling Ball Toss 🧭🥾 🏃
- **Supplies:** Soft ball
- **How to play:** Kids stand in a circle. The teacher calls a book, and each catch adds one letter ("G" → toss → "E" → toss…). If someone misses a letter, restart the word.
- **Learning goal:** Spelling sequence

### 14. Letter Hunt 🐑 🏃
- **Supplies:** Big letter cards hidden around the room
- **How to play:** "Find the letter that starts GENESIS!" Kids hunt and bring back the G. Say the sound together.
- **Learning goal:** First letters and sounds (pre-readers)

### 15. Sky Writing 🐑🧭 🪑
- **How to play:** Everyone "writes" the book name in the air with a big finger while spelling it aloud. Then on a partner's back, then on their palm.
- **Learning goal:** Multisensory spelling

### 16. Hangman (Church Version: "Build the Ark") 🧭🥾🕊️ 🪑
- **How to play:** Classic letter guessing, but each wrong guess adds a piece to a boat drawing. Guess the book or word before the ark is finished.
- **Learning goal:** Spelling patterns

## C. Trivia & Story Games

### 17. Bible Quiz Show 🧭🥾🕊️ 🪑
- **Supplies:** Trivia from the phase file, a bell or buzzer, a scoreboard
- **How to play:** Teams take turns choosing a tier (⭐ 1pt, ⭐⭐ 2pt, ⭐⭐⭐ 3pt). Correct answers score. Steals are allowed for half points.
- **Learning goal:** Story knowledge

### 18. True or False Stand-Up 🐑🧭🥾 🏃
- **How to play:** Read a statement. Kids **stand** for true and **sit** for false (or touch head / touch toes for little ones). Everyone plays every round.
- **Learning goal:** Facts

### 19. Who Am I? 🧭🥾🕊️ 🪑
- **How to play:** The teacher (or a kid) gives three clues about a Bible person, from hard to easy. Teams guess after each clue, and fewer clues means more points.
- **Example:** "I lived in a palace but was born a slave's son… I saw a burning bush… I led God's people out of Egypt." → **Moses**
- **Learning goal:** People of the Bible

### 20. Charades / Bible Pictionary 🧭🥾🕊️ 🏃
- **Supplies:** Slips with stories or people
- **How to play:** Act out or draw the story. The team guesses within 60 seconds.
- **Learning goal:** Story recall

### 21. Story Sequence Race 🐑🧭🥾 🪑
- **Supplies:** 4–6 picture cards (or sentence strips) of a story's events
- **How to play:** Teams put the story in order.
- **Learning goal:** Story order + retelling

### 22. Bible Jeopardy 🥾🕊️ 🪑
- **Supplies:** A grid on a whiteboard or slide: categories × 100–500 points
- **Categories:** "Books," "People," "Places," "Who Said It?," "Memory Verse," "Spell It"
- **Learning goal:** Mixed review (great for a Week 5 Review Sunday)

### 23. Bible Bingo 🐑🧭🥾 🪑
- **Supplies:** Bingo cards with book names (or picture symbols for little ones) and markers
- **How to play:** The caller reads a clue ("The book about the very beginning") and kids mark the answer.
- **Learning goal:** Matching books to summaries

### 24. Memory Verse Scramble 🧭🥾🕊️ 🪑
- **Supplies:** The verse written one word per card
- **How to play:** Teams race to put the verse in order, then say it together.
- **Learning goal:** Memorizing Scripture

### 25. Verse Pop 🐑🧭 🏃
- **Supplies:** Balloons with verse words inside on slips
- **How to play:** Pop the balloons, collect the words, and assemble the verse. (Check for latex allergies; use paper bags to "pop" instead if needed.)
- **Learning goal:** Verse memory

### 26. Snowball Questions 🧭🥾🕊️ 🏃
- **Supplies:** Scrap paper
- **How to play:** Kids write a question about the story, crumple it, and have a 30-second "snowball fight." Each kid picks one up and answers it.
- **Learning goal:** Kids create their own review (great for Trailblazers/Ambassadors)

## D. Calm-Down Games (For End of Class)

### 27. Bible Book "I Spy" 🐑🧭 🪑
"I spy a book that starts with J and has a giant in it…" (1 Samuel, David & Goliath)

### 28. Whisper Down the Lane (Telephone) 🐑🧭🥾 🪑
Whisper the memory verse down the line, then see if it arrives correctly.

### 29. Thumbs Up, Bible Books 🧭🥾 🪑
Like "Heads Up, Seven Up," but the guesser must say a book name before guessing.

## E. Teen Favorites (Ambassadors)

### 30. Bible Escape Room 🥾🕊️
Lock a box with a 3- or 4-digit combo. Clues come from Bible references (e.g., "How many books of Law?" = 5, "Chapter of the Love Chapter in 1 Corinthians" = 13). Takes 20–30 min, ideal for Week 5.

### 31. Speed Overview 🕊️
Each teen gets 60 seconds to explain a book (who, what, where Jesus shows up). Others vote on the clearest explanation.

### 32. Debate Corner 🕊️
Pose a question from the phase's "Big Questions." Two teams prepare 3 minutes and present, then the teacher summarizes the biblical answer.

### 33. Teach-Back 🕊️
Each teen prepares a 3-minute mini-lesson and delivers it to the Little Lambs or Explorers. This is the best way to learn!
