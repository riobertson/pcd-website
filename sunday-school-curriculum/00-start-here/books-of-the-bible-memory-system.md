# Books of the Bible Memory System

How every child memorizes, spells, reads, and finds all **66 books of the Bible** in 12 months.

---

## 1. The Big Picture: The Bible Is a Library

Teach this on Day 1 and repeat it every month:

> **The Bible is ONE book made of 66 little books, like a library on a shelf.**
> It has **2 big parts**: the **Old Testament** (39 books, before Jesus was born) and the **New Testament** (27 books, Jesus and His church).
> Trick to remember: **3 and 9 = 39. 3 × 9 = 27.** Together they make **66**!

### The 10 "Shelves" (Sections) of the Bible Library

Each section has a color, which is used on the flashcards, the classroom "Bible Bookshelf" wall, and the printables.

| # | Section | Books | Color | Hand sign | Kid meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Law** | 5 | 🟥 Red | Two flat hands like stone tablets | "God's rules and the beginning" |
| 2 | **History** (OT) | 12 | 🟧 Orange | Point behind you ("long ago") | "What happened to God's people" |
| 3 | **Poetry & Wisdom** | 5 | 🟨 Yellow | Hand on heart | "Songs and smart sayings" |
| 4 | **Major Prophets** | 5 | 🟩 Green | Big megaphone hands | "God's BIG messengers (long books)" |
| 5 | **Minor Prophets** | 12 | 🟦 Light Blue | Small megaphone (one hand) | "God's mini messengers (short books)" |
| 6 | **Gospels** | 4 | 🟪 Purple | Arms make a cross | "Good News about Jesus" |
| 7 | **History** (NT): Acts | 1 | 🟫 Brown | Hands blow like wind (the Holy Spirit) | "The church is born" |
| 8 | **Paul's Letters** | 13 | 🔵 Dark Blue | Write on your palm | "Letters from Paul" |
| 9 | **General Letters** | 8 | ⚪ Gray | Open an envelope | "Letters from other leaders" |
| 10 | **Prophecy**: Revelation | 1 | 🟡 Gold | Crown on your head | "Jesus wins forever!" |

**Section count chant** (clap on each number):
> "Law has **5**, History **12**, Poetry **5**, Prophets **5 and 12**!
> Gospels **4**, Acts is **1**, Paul wrote **13**, more letters **8**, Revelation **1**: Sixty-six and DONE!"

## 3. The Cumulative Song

Each phase file contains its own verse, set to a familiar tune, with hand motions. Over the year, the verses join into **one big song**. Sing every verse learned so far at the start of each class.

For a single song that covers all 66 books at once, many churches use a well-known "Books of the Bible" tune (several are free on YouTube). Search **@SharefaithKids** for "books of the Bible song," and preview it first. Use whichever version your church already knows. The phase verses still teach the motions and sections.

### Section-by-section chant (no tune needed, use a steady clap)

> **LAW** (5): Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy!
> **HISTORY** (12): Joshua, Judges, Ruth, First and Second Samuel, First and Second Kings, First and Second Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther!
> **POETRY** (5): Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs!
> **MAJOR PROPHETS** (5): Isaiah, Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel!
> **MINOR PROPHETS** (12): Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi!
> **GOSPELS** (4): Matthew, Mark, Luke and John!
> **HISTORY** (1): Acts!
> **PAUL'S LETTERS** (13): Romans, First and Second Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, First and Second Thessalonians, First and Second Timothy, Titus, Philemon!
> **GENERAL LETTERS** (8): Hebrews, James, First and Second Peter, First, Second and Third John, Jude!

## 5. Classroom "Bible Bookshelf" Wall

Build a giant paper bookshelf on a wall with **10 shelves (one per section)**, each in its section color.

- Cut 66 "book spines" from colored cardstock (tall rectangles). Write one book name on each.
- When the class learns a book, a child **adds its spine to the shelf** in order.
- By December, the whole library is full: a visual celebration of the year!
- **Bonus:** Add a small picture symbol to each spine (from the phase file's "picture symbol").

## 6. Finding Books in a Real Bible ("Sword Drills")

> "For the word of God is alive and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword…" (Hebrews 4:12, NIV)

**How to teach:**
1. **Little Lambs:** Find the *middle* of the Bible (roughly Psalms). Find the *start* (Genesis) and the *big split* (Matthew).
2. **Explorers:** Use the Table of Contents, then try without it. Learn: "Old Testament is the front ¾, New Testament is the back ¼."
3. **Trailblazers & Ambassadors:** Timed Sword Drills.

**Sword Drill rules:**
1. "**Attention!**": Bibles closed, held at side.
2. "**Draw swords!**": Bibles held up flat, closed, hands on covers.
3. Leader calls a reference ("**Romans 8:28**") and repeats it.
4. "**CHARGE!**": Kids race to find it. First to find it **stands and reads it aloud**.
5. Points for the first three finders. Rotate so the same kid doesn't always win.

**Book/chapter/verse:** Teach "**John 3:16** = Book **John**, chapter **3** (the big number), verse **16** (the little number)."

## 7. Mastery Check-offs by Age Group

Use these at the end of each phase and in the final review. Record on the class chart and in each child's Bible Passport.

| Level | 🐑 Little Lambs | 🧭 Explorers | 🥾 Trailblazers | 🕊️ Ambassadors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Say** | Sing the song with the class | Say this phase's books alone | Say *all books so far* in order alone | Say all books so far in order + by section, alone, under 2 min by year end |
| **Spell** | Name the first letter of each new book | Spell this phase's books | Spell all books so far | Spell all books so far (written test) |
| **Read** | "Read" the flashcard picture and point to the word | Read the name on a flashcard | Read the book's key verse aloud | Read a passage and explain it |
| **Find** | Point to the Old or New Testament | Find the book using the Table of Contents | Find it in under 30 sec without the contents page | Win a Sword Drill round |
| **Know** | Tell one story from the phase | One-sentence summary per book | Summary + one key person per book | Explain how each book points to Jesus |

## 8. Supporting Learners

- **Non-readers / early readers:** Use picture symbols, songs and motions, and "first-letter" spelling only.
- **Dyslexia or reading struggles:** Offer oral spelling, letter tiles or magnetic letters instead of writing, plus extra time. Never time them in front of others.
- **ADHD / high energy:** Pair memory work with movement (hopscotch spelling, toss-and-say).
- **English learners:** Pair with a buddy, use pictures, and celebrate the book names in their first language too.
- **Special needs:** Adapt the goal (e.g., recognize 5 books by picture) and celebrate progress. **Every child earns the badge for their personal best.**
