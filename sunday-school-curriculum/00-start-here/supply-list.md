# Supply List

## One-Time Setup (Buy Once for the Year)

### Every room
- [ ] Bibles for the age level (see [program overview](program-overview.md#8-recommended-bibles--resources)): at least one per child for Explorers and up
- [ ] Picture story Bible (Little Lambs)
- [ ] TV, projector or laptop with speakers, plus reliable Wi-Fi
- [ ] Whiteboard or easel pad with markers
- [ ] Pocket chart (for flashcards)
- [ ] Carpet squares or floor mats (Little Lambs and Explorers)
- [ ] Bell, buzzers or squeaky toys (trivia)
- [ ] Soft foam balls (2–3) and a beach ball
- [ ] Bean bags (4–6)
- [ ] Painter's tape (floor games; won't damage floors)
- [ ] Timer
- [ ] Bluetooth speaker for music
- [ ] Storage bins labeled by phase

### Memory & recognition
- [ ] Cardstock in the 10 section colors: red, orange, yellow, green, light blue, purple, brown, dark blue, gray, gold (for flashcards and the Bible Bookshelf wall)
- [ ] Laminator + pouches (makes flashcards last all year)
- [ ] Bible Passport booklets: print from [printables/bible-passport.html](../printables/bible-passport.html)
- [ ] Stamp pad + stamps, or sticker sheets for passports
- [ ] 12 phase badges (stickers, pins or patches) × number of kids
- [ ] Lanyards or felt sashes to display badges (optional)
- [ ] Star stickers
- [ ] Certificate paper (for graduation)

### Craft basics (restock monthly)
- [ ] Crayons, washable markers, colored pencils
- [ ] Glue sticks, white school glue
- [ ] Kid scissors (+ left-handed pairs)
- [ ] Construction paper, white printer paper, cardstock
- [ ] Paper plates, paper bags, craft sticks, cotton balls
- [ ] Pipe cleaners, pom-poms, yarn, googly eyes
- [ ] Stickers (assorted Bible or nature themes)
- [ ] Hole punch, stapler, tape
- [ ] Baby wipes and paper towels

### Spelling
- [ ] Magnetic letters or letter tiles (2–3 sets)
- [ ] Small whiteboards + dry-erase markers (one per Explorer)
- [ ] Large letter cards A–Z (Human Spelling game)

## Monthly Supplies

Each phase file lists exact supplies for its object lessons, games and crafts. At the start of each month:

1. Read the phase's **Weekly Lessons**, **Games** and **Activities & Crafts** sections.
2. Make a shopping list from them.
3. Print that phase's printables from `printables/phase-NN-printables.html` (open it in a browser and print).

## Approximate Budget (per room, per year)

| Item | Approx. cost |
|---|---|
| Bibles (10 kids) | $60–$150 |
| Craft supplies | $150–$300 |
| Games & equipment | $50–$100 |
| Badges, stickers, passports, certificates | $50–$120 |
| Snacks for celebrations | $60–$150 |
| **Total** | **≈ $370–$820** |

*(Costs vary. Ask the congregation to donate craft supplies and sponsor a phase.)*
