# YouTube Video Guide

Three YouTube channels are used throughout all 12 phases. Each phase file lists the videos for each week, with search terms and direct channel-search links.

| Channel | Link | What it offers | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 📺 **Sharefaith Kids** | [youtube.com/@SharefaithKids](https://www.youtube.com/@SharefaithKids) | Animated Bible stories, kids' lessons and songs | 🐑 Little Lambs (4–6) and 🧭 Explorers (7–10) |
| 📺 **Bible Journey TV** | [youtube.com/@BibleJourneyTV](https://www.youtube.com/@BibleJourneyTV) | Bible story videos that walk through the Bible narrative | 🧭 Explorers (7–10) and 🥾 Trailblazers (11–13) |
| 📺 **BibleProject** | [youtube.com/@bibleproject](https://www.youtube.com/@bibleproject) | Animated **book overview** videos for all 66 books, plus theme videos | 🥾 Trailblazers (11–13) and 🕊️ Ambassadors (14–17). Explorers can watch with pauses. |

---

## How Videos Fit Each Sunday

- **Time slot:** 10 minutes (0:25–0:35 in the weekly schedule).
- **Rotation:** Each week, each age group watches the channel that fits them best, as listed in the phase file. With combined rooms, show the younger-friendly video to everyone, and send the BibleProject video home for teens.
- **BibleProject book overviews:** BibleProject has an **"Overview"** video for every book of the Bible (longer books like Genesis, Exodus, Isaiah, Matthew, Luke and Acts are split into two parts). These are the backbone for Trailblazers and Ambassadors: show the overview in the first week a book is introduced.

## How the Links Work

Video links can change, so instead of individual video links, the phase files give you **channel search links** like:

```
https://www.youtube.com/@bibleproject/search?query=Overview+Genesis
https://www.youtube.com/@SharefaithKids/search?query=Noah
https://www.youtube.com/@BibleJourneyTV/search?query=Moses
```

Click the link to see that channel's videos on the topic, then **pick the one that best fits your class and time slot**. Write the exact title and link in your lesson notes (there's a space for this in the [weekly session template](weekly-session-template.md)).

> 💡 **Build a playlist:** At the start of each phase, the Media Helper creates a YouTube playlist (e.g., "Purpose Kids – Phase 3") with the chosen videos in order. This saves time on Sunday and gives parents one link to share at home.

## Video Safety & Classroom Checklist

- [ ] **Preview the whole video** (including the end) before showing it.
- [ ] Turn **autoplay OFF** and hide comments and suggestions (use full screen).
- [ ] Be ready to skip ads, or queue the video past any ad before class starts.
- [ ] Check the volume and screen in the room before kids arrive.
- [ ] Give kids a **"Look For" question** before pressing play.
- [ ] Ask **2 follow-up questions** afterward (provided in every phase file).
- [ ] If a video contains anything that doesn't fit your church's teaching or your kids' ages, **skip it** and choose another.

## Watching Tips by Age

| Group | Tip |
|---|---|
| 🐑 Little Lambs | Keep videos under 5–6 minutes. Sit close, and pause to ask "Who is that?" Do the motions during songs. |
| 🧭 Explorers | Give them a "Look For" card with 2–3 things to spot. Pause once in the middle. |
| 🥾 Trailblazers | Hand out a simple note sheet: *Who? What happened? What does it teach about God? Where's Jesus?* |
| 🕊️ Ambassadors | Watch the full BibleProject overview, then discuss: *What surprised you? What's the main theme? How does this book fit the whole Bible story?* |

## Using the Videos at Home

Every phase's **Take-Home Family Page** lists the month's videos so families can re-watch them together. Encourage parents to watch the BibleProject overview *with* their teenager and talk about it.

## Copyright Note

Show videos by streaming them from YouTube during class. Don't download, re-upload or edit them unless the creator's terms allow it. Some ministries offer church licenses or downloadable lesson packs on their own websites (Sharefaith Kids, for example, sells full curriculum materials), so check their sites for extras.
