# Program Overview: "Through the Bible in 12 Months"

**A children's and youth Sunday school program for ages 4–17**
Purpose Church Kids & Youth Ministry

---

## 1. Vision & Goals

> "Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path." (Psalm 119:105, NIV)

Our goal is for every child, from age 4 to 17, to:

1. **Understand** the Bible's one big story, from Genesis to Revelation, in the simplest words possible.
2. **Memorize** all 66 books of the Bible, in order, by section.
3. **Spell and read** the name of every book (each age at its own level).
4. **Meet Jesus** on every page. The whole Bible points to Him.
5. **Love God's Word** because Sunday school is fun: games, coloring, songs, trivia, crafts and videos.

### The One Big Story (teach this every phase)

The Bible is **one big story about God rescuing His people and making everything new**. We use five words, each with a hand motion, that kids repeat all year:

| Word | Hand motion | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **CREATION** | Hands make a big circle (the world) | God made everything good. |
| **FALL** | Hands drop down fast | People chose sin and broke the world. |
| **PROMISE** | Pinky-promise with your own hands | God promised a Rescuer. |
| **JESUS** | Point to palms (nail marks) | Jesus came, died and rose again to save us. |
| **FOREVER** | Arms stretched up high | Jesus is coming back to make everything new. |

## 3. Age Groups

Every lesson is written for one shared Bible story, taught at four levels. With a small church you can combine groups (see "Small Church Setup" below).

| Group | Ages | Grades (approx.) | How they learn | What "mastery" looks like by year end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🐑 **Little Lambs** | 4–6 | Pre-K – K | Pictures, songs, hand motions, coloring, short stories, repeat-after-me | Sing the books song with motions; name the 10 sections; recognize the first letter of many books; tell 12 Bible stories in their own words |
| 🧭 **Explorers** | 7–10 | 1st – 4th | Stories, simple trivia, games, reading short verses, spelling | Say **and spell** all 66 books in order; find any book in their Bible; one-sentence summary of key books |
| 🥾 **Trailblazers** | 11–13 | 5th – 7th | "Why" questions, maps, timelines, looking up verses, teamwork | Say all 66 in order with sections; one-sentence summary of **every** book; place major people on a timeline |
| 🕊️ **Ambassadors** | 14–17 | 8th – 12th | Big themes, worldview and apologetics, real-life application, leading others | Everything above, plus explain how each section points to Jesus; lead a younger group's game or memory drill; give a 2-minute overview of the whole Bible |

**Ambassadors as helpers:** Teens are invited to serve as "Junior Leaders" for one rotation (game, memory drill or craft) in a younger room at least once per month. This builds leadership and reinforces their own learning.

## 4. The Sunday Session (75 minutes)

**Class meets on the 2nd and 4th Sundays of every month.** Each class covers **two lessons** from the phase, so there are two shorter stories instead of one long one. Every class follows the same rhythm, so kids know what to expect and volunteers can step in easily. A printable version is in [weekly-session-template.md](weekly-session-template.md).

| Time | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | 🎵 **Welcome & Book Chant** | Greet kids by name, take attendance, sing the cumulative Books of the Bible song with motions (4th Sunday: add a 2-minute recap of the 2nd Sunday) |
| 0:08–0:20 | 📖 **Story 1** | Object-lesson hook + story script for the first lesson |
| 0:20–0:28 | 📺 **Video** | One video from @SharefaithKids, @BibleJourneyTV or @bibleproject, chosen by age group |
| 0:28–0:40 | 📖 **Story 2** | Story script for the second lesson |
| 0:40–0:48 | 💬 **Small-Group Questions** | One question from each lesson for your age group, plus "Where did we see Jesus today?" |
| 0:48–0:58 | 🎮 **Game** | An active game that reinforces book names or the stories |
| 0:58–1:10 | 🖍️ **Activity / Coloring / Craft** | Coloring page, craft, word puzzle or trivia |
| 1:10–1:15 | ✅ **Memory & Spelling Check + Prayer** | Quick check-offs, stars, closing prayer (4th Sunday: this becomes the 11-minute End-of-Phase Review with Passport stamps and badges) |

Each phase file has a ready-made **"2nd & 4th Sunday Plan"** with these run sheets filled in for that month's lessons, games and crafts.

**Shorter service? (45-minute version):** Chant (5) → Story 1 (8) → Story 2 (8) → Video (6) → Questions (5) → Game *or* Craft (8) → Check + Prayer (5).

## 5. Our Calendar: 2nd & 4th Sundays

### Inside each phase (month)

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| **2nd Sunday** | Introduce the phase (Big Idea, new books, new song verse) + **Lessons 1 & 2**. Spelling list and Take-Home Family Page go home. |
| 1st & 3rd weeks (home) | **Between Sundays:** families sing the song, practice spelling, read the passages and watch the video the class didn't see (listed in each phase file). |
| **4th Sunday** | 2-minute recap + **Lessons 3 & 4** + **End-of-Phase Review**: check-offs, Bible Passport stamp, phase badge. |
| 5th Sunday (if any) | No class. If your church wants an extra session, use the phase's optional "Review & Fun Sunday." |

### The year at a glance (January 2027 start)

| Phase | Month | 2nd Sunday (Lessons 1 & 2) | 4th Sunday (Lessons 3 & 4 + review) | Calendar notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | February | Feb 14 | Feb 28 | Feb 14 is Valentine's Day: "God's love" tie-in |

That's **24 classes a year**. Starting in a different month or year? Keep the same order of phases and use the 2nd and 4th Sundays of each month.

**Missed a Sunday?** Because classes are two weeks apart, a child who misses one class misses a month of content. Teachers should send the Take-Home Family Page and the videos to absent families, and let kids do their check-offs at the next class.

---|---|
| Week 1 | Introduce the phase: Big Idea, new books, new song verse, first story |
| Week 2 | Story 2: add motions, spelling list goes home |
| Week 3 | Story 3: trivia warm-up, spelling relay |
| Week 4 | Story 4 + **End-of-Phase Review**: check-offs, Bible Passport stamp, phase badge |
| Week 5 (if the month has one) | **Review & Fun Sunday**: big review game, catch-up check-offs, celebration snack |

## 6. Recognition System

- **🛂 Bible Passport:** Each child gets a passport booklet (see [printables](../printables/bible-passport.html)). They earn a stamp or sticker for each phase completed.
- **🏅 Phase Badges:** 12 badges, one per phase (described in each phase file). Use stickers, pins or iron-on patches on a lanyard or sash.
- **⭐ Weekly Stars:** Stars for attendance, bringing a Bible, saying the memory verse, and spelling check-off.
- **🎓 Year-End Graduation:** Certificates by level ("Bible Explorer," "66 Books Champion," etc.) at the Phase 12 celebration.

**Fairness rule:** Every child can earn every badge. Mastery is measured *against their own age level*, and teachers may adapt for learning differences. Effort counts.

## 7. Staffing

| Role | Count | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Children's Ministry Director | 1 | Oversees program, schedules volunteers, orders supplies, communicates with parents |
| Lead Teacher (per room) | 1 per age group | Teaches story, leads discussion |
| Assistant / Helper | 1+ per room | Attendance, crafts, check-offs, bathroom escorts |
| Media Helper | 1 (shared) | Queues videos, manages screens and sound |
| Junior Leaders (Ambassadors) | Rotating | Lead games and memory drills in younger rooms |

**Safety:** Follow Purpose Church's child safety policy: background-checked adults, two-adult rule, check-in and check-out, allergy list posted in each room.

### Small Church Setup

- **2 rooms:** Room A = Little Lambs + Explorers (ages 4–10); Room B = Trailblazers + Ambassadors (ages 11–17).
- **1 room:** Teach the story together, then split tables by age for questions and activities. Ambassadors lead the younger tables.
